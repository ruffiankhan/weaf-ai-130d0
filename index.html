What is an AI voice agent?

An AI receptionist that answers your business phone in a natural voice, 24/7. It answers common questions, qualifies callers, books appointments and follows up missed calls, then hands anything complex to you. I've set these up for businesses in Sydney.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Most people I work with are business owners and managers. You tell me how the work gets done today and I turn it into a plain-English plan.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Small and medium businesses in Melbourne, Sydney and across Australia: trades and home services, clinics, professional services and B2B companies.

Online or in person?

Sessions run over video, so you can book from anywhere in Australia. I'm based in Point Cook in Melbourne's west, working with businesses in Werribee, Williams Landing, Hoppers Crossing, Altona, Footscray and across Melbourne.

Is it safe to put my business data into AI tools?

It depends on the tool and the data. Every plan includes choosing tools whose data handling suits your obligations under the Australian Privacy Act, and keeping sensitive customer information out of consumer AI apps.
