What does an AI consultant do for a small business?

The job is to turn "we should be using AI" into a few specific changes that save time or win more work. In practice:

  • Find the work. Map where hours go and where leads slip: unanswered calls, slow replies, double data entry, chasing invoices.
  • Pick the approach. Use features you already pay for first, then connector tools such as Zapier, Make or n8n, and AI models only where text or voice needs handling.
  • Build and test. Set it up, run it next to the manual process, and fix what breaks.
  • Hand over. Document it and show your team how it works, so you're not dependent on the consultant.

Common first projects: an AI voice agent or AI receptionist, missed-call text-back with a booking link, enquiries logged into a CRM with a drafted reply, and meeting notes summarised and filed.

What should I ask before hiring an AI consultant?

  • Can you show me something you've built that's running in a real business now?
  • Is the price fixed, and exactly what does it include?
  • Who owns the accounts, prompts, workflows and data when we're done?
  • What will the ongoing software and usage costs be, and who pays them?
  • Where will my customer data be stored, and how does this fit the Privacy Act?
  • What happens when something breaks, and who fixes it?
  • Do you earn commissions on any tools you recommend?
  • How will we measure whether it worked?

Red flags: a big "AI transformation" proposal before anyone has looked at how you work, vague hourly billing with no scope, and accounts set up in the consultant's name rather than yours.
